About the Club

Who we are, what we stand for, and how we serve Milpitas.

Our Purpose

Serving the community. Building leaders.

The primary purpose of our club is to serve the community while providing students with valuable opportunities for community service. We focus on volunteering and leadership development, primarily through programs at Christ Community Church of Milpitas (C3 Milpitas).

Activities include participation in sports camps, day camps, youth mentoring, and counseling support for younger students — designed to build leadership skills, foster teamwork, and promote community involvement.

Open to all students. This is NOT a strictly Christian club. Membership is open to all MHS students regardless of religion, ethnicity, gender, or sexual orientation.

Officers

Higher Standard for Leaders

Officers are held to a higher bar:

  • Participate in all mandatory summer programs
  • Attend ALL club meetings (excused only with valid reason)
  • Participate in 75% of all club activities
  • Fulfill their specific officer duties consistently
  • Represent the club at CLOG Council and school events
Why join as an officer? You get real leadership experience running programs that impact the wider Milpitas community — not just a title.
