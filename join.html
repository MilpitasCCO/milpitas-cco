FAQ

Common Questions

Do I have to be Christian to join?
No — this is not a strictly Christian club. Membership is open to all MHS students regardless of religion, ethnicity, gender, or any other background. The only requirements are a 2.5 GPA and being a current MHS student.
Is there a membership fee?
No. Membership is completely free. There are no dues, no fees, and no costs associated with joining or participating in events.
When do you meet?
We meet every other Thursday during the lunch period at Milpitas High School. Specific room details will be shared through Google Classroom once you join.
Do volunteer hours count toward graduation?
Yes. Volunteer work with our club counts toward MHS's 20-hour community service graduation requirement. Active members can also qualify for the Maroon Cord through logged service hours.
How do I become an officer?
You need to be an active member for at least one semester and have volunteered for multiple events. Officer applications open in early September. The President and VP review applications and select candidates. Some roles are semester-long, others are year-long.
What if I can't make every meeting?
That's okay — members just need to maintain a 50% attendance rate and participate in at least one volunteer activity per month. If you have a conflict, just let the officers know.
